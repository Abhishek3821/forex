# Solution page imagery

These three images were generated using the built-in image generation tool and saved in this directory for the VTX Prime website. They are illustrative scenes, not photographs of actual employees, client premises or operational security systems. The platform, phone, operations and connectivity graphics are native React/CSS/SVG previews with labelled sample data.

## Saved assets

- `src/assests/forex/trading-workspace.png` — Forex Broker hero and selected editorial backgrounds.
- `src/assests/forex/security-infrastructure.png` — security benefit and technology expertise imagery.
- `src/assests/forex/team-collaboration.png` — illustrative operations and collaboration imagery.

## Prompts used

### trading-workspace.png

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: hero photograph for a premium dark navy forex broker technology website. Primary request: realistic editorial photograph of a professional at a multi-monitor trading workstation in a modern office overlooking a city at blue hour, viewed from behind and slightly to the side. Two large monitors show subtle generic candlestick charts and market data without legible brand names. Calm professional atmosphere, deep navy shadows, soft cyan screen light, natural skin and fabric texture, restrained amber city lights. Landscape composition 3:2, the workstation and person fill the frame naturally, no promotional text, no logos, no watermark. This is an illustrative technology scene, not a photograph of a specific real company or person.

### security-infrastructure.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: editorial image for a forex brokerage technology website. Create a premium photorealistic 3D visual of a transparent sapphire-blue cybersecurity shield with a small luminous cyan lock floating above a finely detailed dark circuit board. Restrained cool navy palette, realistic glass reflections, crisp fine circuit traces, soft atmospheric blue light, cinematic landscape composition 3:2. Beautiful sophisticated financial infrastructure concept. No text, no logos, no watermark. Avoid cartoon style and excessive neon.

### team-collaboration.png

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: editorial photo for a financial technology services website. Create a natural realistic photograph of three diverse adult software and operations professionals collaborating at a modern office desk, reviewing a laptop and a large monitor showing generic analytics and subtle dark charts. Candid side view, professional business casual clothes, modern glass office with cool natural window light, navy and slate tones and a few warm highlights. Landscape composition 3:2, sophisticated and believable. No visible company logos, no legible text, no watermark. Illustrative fictional people rather than a specific real team.
